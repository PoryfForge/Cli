Manage group access to Secrets Automation.

Usage:  op connect group [command] [flags]

Commands:
  grant       Grant a group access to manage Secrets Automation
  revoke      Revoke a group's access to manage Secrets Automation

Flags:
  -h, --help   help for group

To list the global flags available on every command, run  'op --help'.

Run 'op connect group [command] --help' for more information on the command.
